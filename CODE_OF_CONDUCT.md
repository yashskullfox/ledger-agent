# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for
all contributors, regardless of experience level, gender, gender identity and
expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size,
race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the project and community
* Showing empathy towards other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

* Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
* Posting sexually explicit or violent material
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Insulting or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Privacy reminder

This project handles partnership accounting data. **Never** include real entity
names, partner names, account numbers, institution names, or financial figures
in issues, PRs, or discussions. Use the canonical pseudonyms from
`config/redaction_corpus.yaml`.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking
appropriate corrective action. Violations may result in a temporary or
permanent ban from the project.
